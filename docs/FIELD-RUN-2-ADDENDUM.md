# Field run 2, addendum: the leg tallies re-run against their own logs

The first pass of field run 2 left the ten leg groups'
37 discharge tallies unverifiable, because the registers
cite the build slot claim file as the run outputs' home
and that file is not banked in fleet-records. The file
stands on this box (the Fleet Runtime owner seat's claim
file, under the ten-new-open-source-repos goal's
hidden_files), and its discharge notes name the legs'
own cargo logs, which also stand on this box under
~/workspace/chat3-<leg>/ beside each leg's staged tree.

The six leg bundles were reassembled with those logs
inside, hardlinked beside the held repo clones, and the
shipped extraction mode was re-run over the same six
addenda, unchanged. The verdicts change in kind:

Five of the six bundles now verify end to end, exit 0.
SY-A69: 5 claims, all verified, each tally matched to
its named log (mutant-run.log records 23 passed and 5
failed; post-restore-run.log records 28 passed and 0
failed). DM-A49: 5 of 5 (control 28 and 1, mutant 23
and 6). DK-A50: 7 of 7 (control 3 and 0, mutants 2 and
1, 1 and 2). MF-A82 and MF-A83: 7 of 7 (setup 30 passed,
mutants 29 and 1, 28 and 2, and MF-A82's parser mutant
0 passed and 1 failed). PC-A73: 5 of 5 (control 1 and 0,
mutants 0 and 1 twice over).

The wayside legs bundle verifies 13 of its 14 claims
and exits 3 on the one: WS-A74's rustfmt sentence ("read
clean at exit code 0") extracts as an exit tally no
bundled log records in comparable form. Its cargo half
verifies against the gate run, and every other wayside
tally verifies against its named log, including the
mutant targets at 90 and 46, 39 and 36, 20 and 12, and
the post-restore databases target at 75 and 0.

So the ten leg groups' discharge claims stand verified
claim by claim against the evidence the discharges
themselves produced, the staging mains in the repos and
the tallies in the legs' own logs. The first pass's
unverifiable verdict was a property of the bundle, not
of the claims, which is the closure-time lesson with
its direction reversed: the evidence existed the whole
time, one directory over, and assembling the bundle at
the evidence (rather than at the report) is what closed
the run.
